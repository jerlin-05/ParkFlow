# ParkFlow

> Smart Parking Management & Real-Time Parking Operations Platform

ParkFlow is a modern parking management platform designed to digitize parking reservations, entry-gate operations, parking sessions, slot management, incident handling, revenue tracking, and operational monitoring.

The platform connects customers, entry employees, security teams, parking owners, and administrators through synchronized dashboards and real-time parking data.

---

## Overview

ParkFlow provides a complete parking workflow:

```text
Customer
   │
   ├── Enter vehicle number
   ├── Enter mobile number
   ├── OTP verification
   ├── Select parking slot
   └── Confirm booking
          │
          ▼
      ParkFlow
          │
          ├── Booking Management
          ├── Slot Allocation
          ├── QR Booking Ticket
          ├── Entry Management
          ├── Parking Session Tracking
          ├── Exit Management
          ├── Incident Detection
          ├── Automatic Reassignment
          └── Revenue Analytics
                    │
                    ▼
       ┌────────────┬──────────────┐
       │            │              │
    Employee      Security       Owner
